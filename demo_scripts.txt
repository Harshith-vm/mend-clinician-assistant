HOW TO USE
  • Paste ONE numbered line at a time into the chat box.
  • Wait for Mend to finish replying, then paste the next line.
  • Click "Start new session" in the sidebar before beginning a new disorder,
    so each demo starts fresh.
  • The bot understands/asks in the first 2–3 turns, then shifts to actively
    helping and walking through a coping step by turns 4–6.
  • Tip: these three are the most common presentations in the datasets
    (Depression, Generalised Anxiety, Panic) and each ends on a concrete solution.

  (Only the PATIENT lines are below — Mend generates every reply live.)


────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
  DEMO 1 — DEPRESSION  (7 turns)
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

1) Honestly I don't even know where to start. For the last few weeks everything just feels grey and heavy. I've lost interest in the things I used to love, and I'm exhausted no matter how much I sleep.

2) Getting out of bed in the morning feels almost impossible. I hit snooze for an hour and just lie there because nothing feels worth doing.

3) I've stopped replying to my friends. I cancelled plans twice this week — I just don't have the energy to pretend I'm okay.

4) A big part of it is this voice in my head telling me I'm failing at everything and letting everyone down.

5) It's affecting my work too. I stare at my screen and can't focus, and then I feel even worse about falling behind.

6) I really want to feel like myself again. What can I actually do to start climbing out of this?

7) That gives me a bit of hope. What is one small thing I could do today to get started?


────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
  DEMO 2 — GENERALISED ANXIETY DISORDER  (7 turns)
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

1) My mind never switches off. From the moment I wake up I'm worrying about a dozen different things — work, money, my family's health — and I just can't seem to stop.

2) It's like my brain always jumps straight to the worst-case scenario. If a friend doesn't text back I immediately assume something terrible has happened to them.

3) It's physical too — my shoulders are always tense, my stomach is in knots, and I've been getting headaches almost every day.

4) I lie awake for hours at night because I can't turn the worrying off, and then I'm exhausted and even more on edge the next day.

5) I know deep down that most of what I worry about never even happens, but knowing that doesn't make the worry actually stop.

6) I'm so tired of feeling wound up all the time. What can I actually do to quiet my mind a bit?

7) Okay, I'd be willing to try that. What's one thing I could do the next time the worrying starts to spiral?


────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
  DEMO 3 — PANIC DISORDER  (7 turns)
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

1) A few times this month I've had these sudden waves of intense fear out of nowhere — my heart pounds, I can't breathe, and I honestly feel like I'm dying.

2) The last one hit me in the middle of a grocery store. I had to drop my basket and rush out to sit in my car until it passed.

3) Now I'm scared of it happening again, so I've started avoiding places where I think I might panic — crowded shops, the bus, big meetings.

4) Even when I'm calm, there's this constant dread in the back of my mind, just waiting for the next attack to hit.

5) My world feels like it's getting smaller and smaller because of this, and I feel embarrassed that I can't just control it.

6) I really need this to stop running my life. What can I do in the moment when I feel one of these coming on?

7) That's really helpful. What's one small step I could take this week to start facing the places I've been avoiding?
